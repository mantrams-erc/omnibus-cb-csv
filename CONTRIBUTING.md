# How to Contribute to the OMnibus

Thank you for considering to contribute to the OMnibus!

For the OMnibus, we prioritize pragmatic, sustainable, and simplified approaches to ensure it is able to adapt to the diverse material it will eventually carry, and to be meaningful for scholarly communities, for diverse communities of local practitioners and for intersecting spaces between these two worlds. A key task is to arrive at a sensible end-of-life stage beyond the MANTRAMS project runtime (2024-2030). The core team of developers is small - whereas the expectations, challenges and possibilities are vast, often highly complex and sometimes contradictory.

Thus, we are focused on creating an infrastructure that can grow (somewhat) organically and a creative, enthusiastic yet reflexive community of contributors, collaborators and co-designers (please see our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/mantrams-erc/omnibus-cb-csv/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and our [list of project members](https://mantrams.eu/team/)).

While we try to be disruptive in academic spaces with our interdisciplinary, de-colonial approach to religious practices and cultural heritage, you may find our tech stack to be somewhat conservative and minimal. This is both on purpose (see e. g., [Minimal Computing in the Digital Humanities](https://dhq.digitalhumanities.org/vol/16/2/index.html)) and an unavoidable result of project resource allocation. As long as you are willing to meet us where we're at, we are very open to big and small ideas in web development and data management as well as artistic and techno-social interventions of any kind. Especially so, if you have a deep knowledge and curiosity in the iconographies, artistic practices and soundscapes of Global Southern Asia.

## Issues

We are informal in using GitHub Issues and welcome you to open one to get in touch.

Here are some tips:

- [How to use GitHub Issues](https://guides.github.com/features/issues/)
- Please focus on clear communication, providing plenty of detail and links so that we can understand the bug or proposal.
- Search the Issues to see if a related report has already been opened (if so add a comment or reaction!).

You can also always reach out via email to any of our [team members](https://mantrams.eu/team/).

## Pull Requests 

OMnibus welcomes [Pull Requests](https://help.github.com/en/articles/about-pull-requests) from outside contributors. 
Please provide plenty of detail in the PR so that the project team fully understands your contribution.

- [How to create a PR](https://help.github.com/en/github/collaborating-with-issues-and-pull-requests/creating-a-pull-request)

## Conventions

- **No AI code** without serious review and editing to ensure proper functionality, ease of maintenance and human-readability.
- **No shallow self promotions.** We engage with serious and sensitive subject matter in contested contexts. We expect integrity and thoughtfullness, not necessarily technical proficiency from contributions.
- **No copyright infringement.** Some creators are less protected in digital spaces. At MANTRAMS, we want to make sure everyone gets credited for their work whenever at all possible.
- The **authority to accept your contribution** lies with the researchers and their participants, not the OMnibus core development team. The latter will only assess technical compatibility and consequences on the OMnibus' functionalities.
