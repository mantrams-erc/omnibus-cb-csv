The installation invites visitors to experience the dhun of the Name as vibration, repetition, and presence. In this setting, the body appears as a medium of mantra, where breath, sound, and heat converge in practices of discipline and transformation.</p>

    <p class="card-text fw-light fs-6 d-none d-lg-block">by Lucy May Constantini</p>
    <p class="card-text fw-light fs-6"><i>Sounding the Goddess</i> is a series of soundscapes edited from field recordings made at two sites in Kerala during October and November 2025. Some come from <i>kaḷaripūja</i> at CVN Kalari Sangham at East Fort in Thiruvananthapuram, in particular the ritual called <i>Bhagavati sēva</i> (offering to the goddess Bhagavati). This elaborate rite is performed on the ninth night of the annual <i>navarātri</i> festival, a high point of the <i>kaḷari</i> year. Other recordings are drawn from the Śrïvidyā rituals offered in the family home of Hindustan Kalari Sangham in Kozhikode. Performed three times a day in the household <i>pūja</i> room, they are, mostly unseen, understood to support, purify and enliven the <i>kaḷari</i>, its activities and community.</p>
